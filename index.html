Practice Real Conversations

Explore real-life English conversation scenarios used in our practical sessions.

Speaker A Hello! How has your week been going so far?
Speaker B It has been quite busy, but productive! I'm focusing on improving my English skills this month.
Speaker A That's great to hear! Practice makes a huge difference over time.
Interviewer Could you tell me a little about your background and key strengths?
Candidate Certainly. I have worked in customer service for two years, where clear English communication was essential.
Interviewer Excellent. How do you handle challenging situations at work?
Teacher Who can explain the main difference between present perfect and simple past tenses?
Student Present perfect links the past to the present, while simple past describes a finished time!
Colleague A Shall we review the proposal outline before finalizing the presentation slides?
Colleague B Yes, let's go over section two to ensure our core message is concise and clear.
Traveler Excuse me, could you direct me to the nearest train station?
Local Of course! Walk straight down this street for two blocks, then turn right.
Person A Hi, my name is Sarah. It's a pleasure to meet you!
Person B Nice to meet you too, Sarah! I'm Alex. Are you new to this academy?
